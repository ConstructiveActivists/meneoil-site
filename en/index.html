Compliance from the first click

In Venezuela's oil sector, compliance is not a detail: it is the ground every transaction stands on.

OFAC

Sanctions screening of every counterparty before we quote.

KYC

Buyers, suppliers and end users identified.

AML

Payments only through regulated banking channels, with source of funds.

Records

Every transaction documented end to end. Your compliance team can ask us for our file under an NDA.

Before an order moves

  • Export controls U.S. sanctions authorization and U.S. export authorization are two separate questions: an OFAC license does not by itself authorize an export. Both are settled first.
  • End use A signed end-use and end-user certificate naming the real end user and site. No military or military-intelligence end use, no diversion, no re-export.
  • Counsel opinion No U.S.-origin goods ship to an oil-sector end user until we hold a written opinion from U.S. sanctions and export-control counsel.
